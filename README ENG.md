# BlueSteelGenesis
### Tooltips
- Right-click on enemies and modules to see tooltips with additional info.

### Godmode
- Godmode can be activated from expedition map interface.
- Activation gives player huge health and a module that deals big damage

### Map nodes
- Normal encounter: fight for money and materials/scraps (that are used to upgrade modules or rest)
- Elite encounter: fight for more money and materials and also a unique module that was used by the enemy (or a random normal one if the unique elite encounters have been exhausted for this stage).
- Treasury: get a random normal module.
- Shop: buy and sell normal modules.
- Respite: recover your health (you can spend collected materials to recover more) and upgrade modules (using the same materials).
- Boss: fight in the end of the stage to go further and also get a golden ticket.
- Black market: spend collected golden ticket for a boss module which are stronger (and weirder) than normal ones.
